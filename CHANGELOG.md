## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-01

First public release. What 0.1 does not do yet is listed in the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md).

### Added

- **Engine.** Workspaces for several clients or brands on one engine, kept apart. One operation registry behind a safety gate: API keys with scopes, approvals, review levels, dry runs, idempotency keys, monthly AI and data budgets, a per-workspace kill switch and an audit log. Jobs and schedules stored in the database, events, signed outgoing webhooks, and notifications to Slack, email or a webhook.
- **One permission model.** The same scopes, workspace boundaries and approval rule on every door and in every job: only a person holding the `approve` scope acts without asking, agents get an approval request, nobody decides their own request, and an approval applies only to what it showed. The local agent's default scopes are `read,write,send,spend`. `mcp --workspace <slug>` binds a session to one workspace. A key never outlives the key that created it, and neither do the webhook URLs it creates. A permanent test asks every operation the same permission questions through every door ([Safety and approvals](docs/concepts/safety-and-approvals.md)).
- **Doors generated from the registry.** An MCP server (stdio, or Streamable HTTP at `/mcp`) with 49 tools in 8 toolsets, a CLI with a command for every operation, and a REST API with an OpenAPI 3.1 document. The CLI and `openoutbound mcp` bridge to a running `serve` automatically. Dry runs answer the same on every door.
- **Storage.** Embedded PGlite for local use (one process at a time, guarded by a lock file that names the process holding it) and Postgres for servers. Secrets encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with key rotation (`openoutbound db reencrypt-secrets`).
- **Agent Skill and Claude Code plugin**, with playbooks for ICP, signals, copywriting, sequences, deliverability, LinkedIn, replies, meetings, the lead file, CRM and compliance. Sessions start with the strategy page and the operating state.
- **AI brain providers.** Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Gemini, any OpenAI-compatible server (presets for Ollama, LM Studio, Groq, DeepSeek, Together and Mistral), the Claude Code and Codex CLIs for personal use, and an agent brain where the connected agent does the AI work. Model tiers, per-task model overrides, a price table for cost tracking, and a backup brain (`ai.fallback_provider`) when the main one fails.
- **Knowledge and strategy.** A knowledge base, offers, ICPs and lessons, bootstrapped from a company website. A strategy page agents read first, a change log with undo for settings, offers, ICPs and campaigns, and change proposals that apply at once or wait for an owner and are compared before and after.
- **Leads.** CSV, XLSX and JSON imports with automatic column mapping, Apollo and Google Maps searches, saved searches on a schedule, ICP fit scores, lists, suppressions, exports, a retention sweep and GDPR forget.
- **Lead file.** Short business facts from replies with their source and expiry, notes, corrections, one history across channels and campaigns, promises in sent replies turned into tasks, and holds on a whole company. The writer and reply drafts get a short "what we know" block.
- **Enrichment.** An email waterfall: the company website, finders (Icypeas, Findymail, Hunter, Prospeo), verifiers (MillionVerifier, Reoon), optional pattern guessing and a catch-all policy. Finds the people named on a company website. System addresses such as noreply@ are never used. A person whose enrichment failed at a provider is marked `provider_failed` and followed up.
- **Research and signals.** Sourced research briefs (Parallel, Exa, Tavily, Firecrawl or a built-in website reader), `partial` with their gaps when a source failed. A catalog of 15 buying signals with scoring and decay, free collectors (website changes, job boards, news, RSS, technology detection), PredictLeads and Crustdata, signals pushed by webhook, custom signals written in plain English, monitors and automations.
- **Campaigns.** Several campaigns per workspace with their own settings, templates, email, LinkedIn and task steps, a writing pipeline with checks and a checker model, previews and teaching, A/B variants with leaders and a confidence figure, a launch checklist and a sequencer that respects schedules, limits and stop rules.
- **Email.** Mailboxes over SMTP and IMAP with passwords or Google and Microsoft OAuth, CSV import, DNS checks (also daily), ramps, daily limits, bounce monitoring with automatic pauses, reply sync, Sent folder reading, one-click unsubscribe and a compliance footer. Campaign email in a real workspace needs a public https base URL, so every email carries a working unsubscribe link.
- **No double sends.** Every email, LinkedIn action and post claims its row before it goes out. A send with no clear answer becomes `unknown`: it is looked up (in the Sent folder for email) or settled by a person (`resolve_unknown`), never blindly repeated, and a late answer is recorded as sent or as a duplicate. One written rule, with a proof table that names the test for each boundary and each failure ([Delivery guarantees](docs/concepts/delivery-guarantees.md)).
- **LinkedIn.** Actions through Unipile with conservative limits, working hours and a ramp; posts through LinkedIn's official API or Unipile. Actions and posts wait while their provider is paused.
- **Inbox and pipeline.** Reply classification with prompt-injection defenses, an action matrix with locked protective rules, reply drafts from the knowledge base, automatic replies only under strict conditions, privacy requests with a deadline, referrals, opportunities, tasks, and threads a person takes over (from the mailbox itself or with `reply_to_thread` action `take_over`).
- **Meetings.** Booking modes per workspace, meeting records from Calendly, Cal.com or any booking tool that can send JSON, or recorded by hand. Booking links carry a hidden per-person code, so a booking still matches the right lead. The engine never confirms a time itself; held, no-show and qualified meetings are counted.
- **Operator tools.** A view per person with the next action and what blocks it, a problems list that names the fix for each problem, the operating state, the next actions, a change feed for agents, and copying a client's setup to another workspace.
- **CRM.** HubSpot, Pipedrive or your own endpoint, or your agent syncs any CRM. Preferences for who syncs and what is written, and a CRM facts door (also a secret webhook) that stops outreach to customers, open deals, owned accounts and do-not-contact records at once.
- **Provider failures.** Every provider call has a timeout and a failure class with one retry rule. A write that may have acted is `outcome_unknown` and never retried on its own, a paid call that lost its answer is not repeated, and partial results keep what was paid for. A rejected key or a used-up quota pauses that provider for the workspace with one `provider_down` problem. Provider errors never carry a key. A conformance test runs every provider call through each failure ([Provider failures](docs/concepts/provider-failures.md)).
- **Readiness.** `workspaces readiness` and the `sending` block of `get_status` say exactly what still stands between a workspace and a real send, each with the command that fixes it, and every blocker is proven by a test to be what the engine holds back. `doctor` checks the whole setup.
- **Reports.** Report types with defined metrics, a pipeline with meetings held, A/B leaders, scheduled reports to Slack or email, and an attention queue.
- **Sandbox.** A deterministic fake world (companies, people, mailboxes, LinkedIn, providers, replies and meeting bookings) and a fake brain with realistic answers, so agents can practice without keys or risk. Its seed and reset only touch sandbox workspaces.
- **Agent evals.** Twelve scenarios that start a fresh engine, give an agent a task, and check the database, the operations it ran and what it told the human. A scripted runner for CI, and runners for the Claude Code CLI, the Codex CLI and the Anthropic API (`pnpm evals`).
- **Deployment and docs.** A Dockerfile and Docker compose with Postgres, a documentation site in `docs/` with generated reference pages, a first-hour guide and a going-live guide tested as written, example agent configs and webhook verification in `examples/`, and brand images rendered from HTML sources with `scripts/render-brand.mjs`.
